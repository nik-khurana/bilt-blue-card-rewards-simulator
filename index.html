ℹ️ Bilt Blue Card Rewards Rules

Way 1: Housing Rewards

Under Way 1, you earn points on your rent payment based on your Everyday Spend-to-Rent Ratio:

  • < 25% ratio: Flat 250 base points.
  • 25% - 49.9% ratio: 0.50x rent multiplier.
  • 50% - 74.9% ratio: 0.75x rent multiplier.
  • 75% - 99.9% ratio: 1.00x rent multiplier.
  • ≥ 100% ratio: 1.25x rent multiplier (max).
  • Rent Day (1st): Earn 2x points on the first $500 of spend (up to 1,000 pts), 1x thereafter.

Way 2: Cash Back Alternative

Under Way 2, instead of earning rent multiplier points, you earn:

  • Standard Points: Everyday spend earns points (2x on 1st up to $500, 1x rest).
  • 4.0% Bilt Cash: Flat 4% cash back on all non-rent everyday card spend.
  • No Rent Points: No rent multiplier bonus points are awarded.

Which should you pick?

If your everyday spend easily reaches 50%-100% of your rent, Way 1 often wins because the rent points bonus is substantial. If your rent is high and you don't spend much on the card, or if you prefer pure cash back over airline/hotel transfer points, Way 2 is the better choice.
